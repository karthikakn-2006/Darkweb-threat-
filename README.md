# 🕵️ DARKTRACE

## AI-Powered Dark Web Threat Actor Intelligence & De-anonymization

> **SIH 2026 | Problem Statement: SIH26151 | Theme: Blockchain & Cybersecurity | Category: Software**

DARKTRACE is an AI-assisted cybersecurity intelligence platform designed to correlate publicly observable and authorized intelligence signals to identify **potential relationships between pseudonymous threat-actor profiles**.

- 🔐 PGP Intelligence

- 🕸️ Relationship Graph Analysis
- 🧠 AI / GNN Link Prediction
- 📊 Confidence Scoring
- 📑 Evidence-Based Dossier Generation

# 🚀 Project Overview

Threat actors frequently use multiple aliases, PGP keys, cryptocurrency wallets, online accounts, domains and infrastructure to hide their identity.

Traditional investigation methods often analyze these indicators separately.

DARKTRACE combines multiple independent signals into a unified relationship graph.
